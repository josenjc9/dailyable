EVIDENCE · ORIGIN · PILOT

Built from one year of paper.
Ready for the first real test.

DailyAble is a deployed proof of concept, not a field-validated service. The software works; the next evidence must come from consent-based use with 5–10 participants at a partner site.

WHERE IT STARTED

It started as a sheet of paper before each shift.

For a year, workers at a sheltered workshop completed a two-minute daily status sheet. Over time, they began noticing the shape of their own days. That is a practitioner observation, not a trial and not a therapeutic claim.

Ask little. Ask daily. Show the person their own pattern. Leave the meaning to them and the professionals around them.

What works now

Remove avoidable digital burden

  • Mobile web by URL or QR; no app store
  • Eight tap-first questions, one at a time
  • Participant and supporter tasks are separated
  • A one-page printable visit summary

What the pilot must add

Test assisted and non-digital entry

  • Report independent and assisted completion separately
  • Measure task success for each access mode
  • Document remediation below 70% success
  • Do not claim this access gap is solved

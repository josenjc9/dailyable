Write down what you can. Blank is fine.

The time fills itself in. Everything else can be left blank — one number is still worth writing down, and your doctor can read the blanks too.

Your own trend

Each line is your own — the readings you took and the meals you wrote down, over the stretch you picked. Gaps are days with nothing written down. Nothing here is compared with anyone else, and nothing here is a diagnosis.

How the days felt

Your own check-in answers over the same stretch: how your energy was, and whether your routine held. The check-in does not ask you to score your mood, so nothing here is scored — these are the answers you picked, placed on a timeline.

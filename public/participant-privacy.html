What a supporter receives

Only after you confirm it, and only these:

  • The observable reason — what changed, in plain words
  • The request you chose to send
  • The permission scope you set, and when it ends
  • A suggested time to follow up with you

What stays out

These never leave your space on their own:

  • Your full record history
  • Raw blood pressure, glucose, medicine and meal entries
  • Any hidden risk score
  • A diagnosis — DailyAble does not make one
  • Automatic notification to any other service

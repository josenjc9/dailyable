ABOUT 2 MINUTES

Eight tap-first questions.

Energy, mood, sleep, eating and drinking, how hard it is to start something, memory or perception, the kind of help wanted, and any numbers measured today. One question at a time, with a visible Continue button and the freedom to stop. Depending on the answers, at most three fixed follow-up questions may appear, and every one of them can be skipped.

Alex reports: energy very low; routine off track.No screening score. No comparison with other people.
COMPUTED, NOT GENERATED

Compared with Alex's own recent days.

Arithmetic counts how often the routine changed, how long lower energy has continued, and whether signals moved together. AI is optional and may only rephrase computed facts.

Lower energy and changed routine fell on the same 6 days.The sentence is traceable to dated entries.
PARTICIPANT CONTROL

A suggestion Alex may decline.

The service suggests one smaller shape for today, shows why, and asks Alex whether Jordan should receive the summary.

Pause or shorten one planned task.Nothing is shared before final confirmation.
CONFIRMED SHARING ONLY

Jordan receives context, not a record dump.

Priority, time running, permitted trend, conversation opener and next review appear together. Sharing level is enforced by the server.

Trend level: the shape can be drawn; real values do not leave the server.Jordan records contacted, scheduled or closed.
THE LOOP CLOSES

Alex can see what happened next.

The follow-up returns to the participant's space with status and next review. Support does not disappear into an internal console.

Jordan recorded contact.Alex still controls whether the connection continues.

WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

Six decisions the demo can prove.

  1. 01Compared with yourself

    No population score, percentile or hidden threshold.

  2. 02Computed, not generated

    AI contributes phrasing only and is off by default.

  3. 03Function before label

    Ask what makes participation possible; no diagnosis is required.

  4. 04Consent enforced by server

    At trend level the real values are never sent.

  5. 05The supporter is also a person

    The workspace includes official caregiver support guidance.

  6. 06One page leaves the screen

    The visit summary is verified to print on one A4 page.
